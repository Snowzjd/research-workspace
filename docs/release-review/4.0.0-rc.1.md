## What still blocks publication

1. No Windows rebuild of Electron 45.4.1 has been made or tested. The old portable ZIP and the 162-pass report are historical only.
2. The packer still does not Authenticode-sign. SmartScreen and the distribution channel are open. Do not describe the future binary as signed.
